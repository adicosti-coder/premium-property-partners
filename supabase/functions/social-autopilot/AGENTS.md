# social-autopilot rules
- `social-autopilot` (daily orchestrator job `social.autopilot`, slots/horizon in its job config) only assigns clean feed-photo drafts of live content to free slots via `_shared/socialAutopilotPlan.ts` (strict property/article rotation across already-scheduled posts, HTTP media check before queuing, „Necesită verificare manuală” flag (legacy wording still recognized) in `last_error` for missing media/Story/Reel/earlier errors), rewrites yield claims in drafts and scheduled posts through `_shared/socialYieldGuard.ts` (approved range for articles/service posts; sale/investment listings show `social_yield_override`, else `roi_percentage` up to the grid cap; `process-scheduled-posts` and `generate-caption` re-apply it), tops up drafts from `_shared/socialLocalExtract.ts` (blog covers only with public URLs) and alerts 48h gaps through the Make relay with direct WhatsApp fallback; publishing stays with `process-scheduled-posts` — keeps the calendar fed with compliant content without a second publish path.
